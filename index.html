Faster Electrical Ink

Creating a E-Ink device which doesnt lack anything in comparison to other lcd/oled devices and outperforms them with every advantage of an e-ink screen.

What is different

A high refresh rate panel. Scrolling, typing and drawing keep up with the hand instead of trailing it.

One device for both jobs. An ereader that is also usable as a tablet, without switching to a backlit screen for anything interactive.

Reflective by nature. No backlight, no flicker, readable in direct sun, days of battery instead of hours.

Open foundation. The work builds on the Modos Labs Glider rather than starting from a closed platform.

Segler stands for fluidity and smoothness, and for speed. It also respects the basis of the project, which is the Modos Labs Glider.
